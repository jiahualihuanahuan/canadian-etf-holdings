Overview

The Wealthsimple Shariah World Equity Index ETF complies with Shariah law and excludes companies deriving more than 5% of their income from alcohol, tobacco, pork-related products, weapons, conventional banking or insurance companies, and adult entertainment. The ETF also excludes companies with excessive leverage. 

WSHR and its underlying index have been certified by a team of Islamic researchers at Ratings Intelligence Partners, and dividend purification information is made available quarterly. 

Companies in the index are weighted by risk, not market capitalization, and companies are screened to perform well on a low-volatility, high-quality multi-factor score.
